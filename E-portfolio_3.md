Artefact #1: Robodebt scheme
Link: https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/system/files/2023-09/rrc-accessible-full-report.PDF

Summary:
The Royal comission into the Robodebt scheme was established in August, 2022 in order to investigate devastating consequences of this scheme on the economy and the public. The scheme used on a process known as "income averaging" to assess income and entitlement to benefit. Moreover, the process assumed that everyone received steady, consistent paycheck each week, which is entirely wrong for casual or seasonal workers (detached from reality). To make it worse, public servants were mostly ignored or discouraged when they raised concerns about it, which the system failed to focus on human element.
